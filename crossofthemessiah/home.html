About crossofthemessiah

Biography, musical background, and ministry service

Following Jesus through musicianship and artistry. I'm a metalcore vocalist looking for a band. I have been in bands that have opened up for the 2007 Rockstar Taste of Chaos Tour with headliners The Used, Senses Fail, Saosin, Chiodos, Aiden, and 30 Seconds To Mars with frontman Jared Leto. Performed with bands such as Evaline, Four Letter Lie, The Color Morale, and Another Hero Dies. Winners of the MySpace Battle of the Bands earning a stage at the 2007 Rockstar Taste of Chaos Tour at The Rave/Eagles Club in Milwaukee, WI on Mar. 17th, 2007. Also, I play bass and acoustic guitar for church, both contemporary and hymnal. I write my own music and incorporate a variety of instruments and styles. Contact me for inquiries and projects. A Follower of the Way, I am an open minded seeker and former atheist. Song Idea 7: On the Rationality of Fairness

The original version of Song Idea 7: On the Rationality of Fairness was fully composed and performed by me without AI. The lyrics and music are of sole human authorship. The version shown here was generated using Suno AI, using only the original lyrics I wrote. These lyrics draw inspiration from Romans 8:6, Exodus 3:14, John 8:58, and René Descartes’ “Cogito, ergo sum.”

Creepin Through the Darkness

Creepin Through the Darkness is a song originally created in 2012 using only my voice and the BOSS RC-30 Loop Station. All audio is of human authorship. In 2024, I reimagined the track in FL Studio 21 using new vocal samples, VST instruments (Flex and CM Drum Hits), and the same melodic structure: F# / E / Eb / Db. The first 73 seconds of the remix are entirely human-produced. Later sections experiment with SUNO AI’s upload feature, generating full AI adaptations from the original human-made recordings.

“O Ye of Little Faith” Vocal Demo

“O Ye of Little Faith” is a vocal demo blending my original music and performances with AI-assisted instrumentation. The first 120 seconds are entirely human-created, featuring my own vocals. Inspired by Matthew 8:26 (KJV), the lyrics were co-written with my wife. Produced in FL Studio, this piece builds on my original recordings, using AI to enhance and expand the musical vision while preserving its spiritual and emotional core.
